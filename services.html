Our Services

Crafted with intention. Tailored to you.

At MASEKO Cape Town, we offer tailored fashion solutions designed to meet your individual needs with style, precision, and excellence.

01 - Bespoke

Custom Garment Design

We bring your vision to life through fully bespoke pieces for special occasions, graduations, formal events, or everyday statement wear. Each garment is made-to-measure and uniquely designed to suit your body, style, and story.

02 - Expert Fit

Tailoring & Alterations

As your trusted "Clothes Doctor," we offer expert alteration services to give your garments the perfect fit and renewed life. From resizing to zip replacements, we handle every detail with care and precision.

03 - Restoration

Repairs & Restorations

We revive worn or damaged garments through professional repairs that extend their lifespan without compromising quality or appearance ideal for sentimental pieces or premium fabrics.

04 - Collection

Ready-to-Wear Pieces

Explore our limited collections of ready-made designs that reflect the bold and modern identity of MASEKO Cape Town. All pieces are rooted in African craftsmanship and urban elegance.

06 - Expertise

Fabric Consultation

Get expert guidance on fabric selection, quality, and care. We help you choose the perfect materials for your project, ensuring durability, comfort, and aesthetic appeal tailored to your vision.

07 - Personal Style

Style Consultation

Discover your unique style with our personalized consultation services. We work with you to identify what works best for your body type, lifestyle, and preferences to create a signature look.

05 - Technical

Pattern Making & Cutting

We provide technical fashion services for fellow designers or students who need help with pattern drafting, sample cutting, or garment construction. Available by request a resource for those who understand the craft.
